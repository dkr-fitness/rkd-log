## Standing Rules

- **Glides:** stop for the day if tingling increases or lingers. Sliders, not stretches.
- **Elbow position:** avoid sustained deep elbow bend and direct pressure on the elbow, day and night (see Float).
- **No trigger point release or hard friction massage** on the right arm.
- **Symptom gate:** pain, plus paresthesia (ring/pinky ulnar, thumb/dorsal radial) and painless weakness — the findings that don't present as pain.
